Chaos Rings II (CR2-U): a fan translation goes here (optional).

It is the game's files the translation changed, as they are in the game's archive:
- message/*.dat (and data/*.dat), with the translated text in English's place;
- images/*.img, any art it redrew.
Put them in a folder named by the language: ru/ for Russian (ru/message/t01_02_c005_0101a.dat, ...).
Any folders inside it are fine; the files are found by their names.

Or drop the .zip, .rar or .7z they come in here. It is unpacked into its own folder on the next
start, and the archive can then go.

It doesn't replace anything. The game's own files stay as they are, and the translation is a
language of its own after the game's, e.g. "Русский" in Options > Language, with English still
there. While it is chosen, its text and its art are read from it. A file it doesn't have is the
game's own, in English.
